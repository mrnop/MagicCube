# Privacy Policy for Magic Cube

**Last updated: December 6, 2025**

Magic Cube ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by Magic Cube.

This Privacy Policy applies to our application named Magic Cube (the "App"). By accessing or using our App, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information processed locally
Our Service is designed to process your photos and images locally on your device.
*   **Images and Photos**: When you use the App to create projects, crop images, or generate magic cubes, the images are processed directly on your device. We do not upload your photos to our servers. Your original photos and the edited versions remain on your device unless you choose to share them using the system's sharing features.

### B. Device and Usage Data
We may collect non-personally identifiable information about your device and how you use the App to help us improve our services.
*   **Device Information**: We may collect information about your mobile device, such as the device type (e.g., iPhone or Android), unique device identifiers (Device ID), operating system, and version.
*   **Usage Data**: We may collect anonymous data about how you interact with the App, such as which screens you view, which templates you use, and the actions you take (e.g., "Project Created", "Image Cropped").

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
*   **To Provide and Maintain the Service**: To allow you to edit photos and create magic cube projects.
*   **To Improve the App**: We use analytics data to understand user preferences and fix bugs.
*   **To Facilitate Sharing**: When you choose to share a project (e.g., as a PDF or images), the App uses your device's native sharing capabilities to send the file to the app or contact you select.

## 3. App Permissions

The App requests the following permissions to function:
*   **Camera**: Required to allow you to take photos directly within the App for your projects.
*   **Storage (Read/Write)**: Required to load images from your gallery and to save your created projects and exported files (PDFs/Images) to your device.
*   **Internet**: May be used for analytics or to check for app updates.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We generally do not share your personal information with third parties. However, we may use third-party services for:
*   **Analytics**: We may use tools to help us analyze usage patterns. These tools may collect information sent by your device, but this data is anonymized.

## 5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Since your personal content (photos) remains on your device, it is protected by your device's security features.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
